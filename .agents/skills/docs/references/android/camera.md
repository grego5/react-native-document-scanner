# Android camera

## Document camera

- The view owns CameraX binding, capture admission and presentation. It borrows the host session. Camera use requires an active, attached foreground view. Preview, analysis and still capture share a viewport.
- Capture output and processing use the settings admitted at the press. Live focus and guide settings govern readiness. Rebinding waits for capture settlement.
- [Shared engine](../engine.md) owns guide acceptance and gate contracts. [Android validation](validation.md) owns hardware checks and trace workflows.

### Focus ownership

- `FocusController` owns lens commands, tap/capture ownership and each scan's whole attempt: the frames that prove its lock, tied to the scan's token, one resend of a lost trigger, its deadline and its release. A release asked for during a still waits for the shot to settle, since cancelling would move the lens mid-shot. The page print target is stored in page coordinates so flow can carry it. Metering fits inside the page. If a box cannot fit, focus uses the frame centre.
- Optical flow only carries the guide's outline and paces its inference. Motion for focus and capture comes from the motion sensors, since flow over soft frames, before focus is found or in the dark, reads motion a still hand does not make: the focus cycle's steady hold and the print spot's pick wait for the gyroscope and accelerometer to read quiet over 300 ms, and the capture's framing checks both sensors per frame interval while its smear check reads the gyroscope. The accelerometer is needed because the gyroscope sees rotation, not a move along or toward the page. A running sensor's missing reading is unknown motion, never quiet. Without the accelerometer the focus cycle falls back to the capture's steadiness and framing judges rotation alone; without a gyroscope the capture's motion checks are off.
- With `focusLock` and live corners enabled, continuous autofocus focuses the page first, aimed at its print, and the scanner runs a focus cycle per steady hold. Continuous autofocus reports focus on a new scene before it reaches the page, and holds a nearer object such as a hand as focused, so its report is never trusted: on a steady, confirmed page its settling starts the camera's own locking scan, which sweeps until the print is sharp and confirms or corrects it, as its reporting no focus does. A press locks whatever it reports, and a press on a held lock shoots at once. The lock also runs deliberately in a dark scene by the auto torch's exposure × ISO signal with no torch or boost lit, or for a cycle step after a failure. A page lock holds until the page is unseen for 1 s, the phone turns about 4 degrees, twice a steady hand's drift, or the motion sensors read motion for 0.6 s, longer than a tremor reads as motion; the next steady hold confirms continuous autofocus anew. The camera's own report decides a lock: a lock that reports no focus fails, and the cycle takes its next step; no sharpness check of the scanner's own judges it. A locked lens still feeds zero shutter lag, whose buffer accepts locked frames. A trigger the camera takes while still in continuous autofocus freezes the lens without a sweep and is then dropped, though CameraX may report it as a lock. A lock therefore counts only once a frame after its trigger reports it in single-shot autofocus, and `FocusController` sends a lost trigger once more without cancelling, with the camera already in single-shot autofocus, and a scanner lock that loses it again fails as a missed lock. Without a page, a centre lock runs only in the dark or when continuous autofocus reports no focus. An eligible auto-torch step gives the cycle one more attempt before it gives up. A brief guide dropout does not release the page lock.
- The cycle owns focus for a press that requires it. `ScannerPress` provides one 8 s uptime budget, with one final autofocus attempt at give-up or near the deadline. A centre lock qualifies only when the press needs no page. Capture needs a fresh post-lock frame and, when required, fresh page evidence. Turning off managed focus hands a waiting press to the regular gate. Lens-setting changes defer during exposure.

### Light and ZSL

- `CameraLightController` owns torch and boost commands and confirmations. Auto torch latches for the run and shares that decision with focus recovery. With latency bound it may light while a press waits. With ZSL bound it waits for capture settlement. Manual torch, boost and exposure changes defer during a pending capture. CameraX keeps only the last set of torch and boost on, so the boost is asked off while the torch is lit and on again once it is off.
- CameraX owns ZSL frame selection. Gated ZSL checks buffer eligibility even with optional focus, subject to the fixed-focus exception. Held-lens focus, torch and sustained white-balance instability can bind latency; darkness without boost does only with the experimental `darkLatency` prop, off by default, since a page press waits for its focus lock and for the newest frame the buffer accepts, which keeps frames from before the scan out of the photo. A buffer-starved capture can trigger one latency fallback for the view lifetime. Other light fallbacks are reconsidered on later bindings.

## Text camera

- `HybridTextCameraView` owns a separate preview and RGBA analysis binding. The shared viewport maps the visible OCR region into the analysis crop. ML Kit receives upright pixels. Optical flow carries the newest agreed outline between recognitions. It tracks the outline's own text, or a stretch of a long line around the aim at most 6 line heights long, since flow's fit rejects points along one thin line and a stretch moves as the whole line does. Features are found and the motion fitted on that text alone, so it fills the area the fit must cover, cropped with a quarter of its length to either side and two line heights above and below from the full frame and kept at up to 448 px, so the strokes stay sharp; the crop changes only when the text nears its edge. A frame flow cannot measure, as a new crop or a smeared frame, moves the highlight by the phone's rotation, which the gyroscope measures with the camera's optics and the applied zoom, for at most 0.3 s since the image last placed it: at reading distance sliding the phone moves the image as much as turning it, and the gyroscope sees only the turn. Past that, the highlight stays in place while the motion sensors read the phone still, since the text has not moved, and is otherwise hidden, since its position is no longer known and the aim may have left the text, which clears an ended reading, as does the region's centre leaving text it was on when read, past half a line's height or a twentieth of its length, and recognition returns to its full rate so the next read that boxes the text places it again.
- Main publishes a per-frame snapshot of region, filter, vote revision, zoom admission and focus session. The analyzer owns voting. Main accepts only current binding and revision results. Closing, interruption or failure clears readings and fails pending reads with `camera/unavailable`. A fresh frame restores readiness on reopen.
- `TextZoom` separates the submitted ratio from camera evidence. Each request clears the reading and pauses recognition and the focus cycle. The current request's completion and matching capture metadata establish the frame boundary. When matching metadata is unavailable or does not arrive within the grace period, admission uses the next frame after the latest observed result. Refusal uses the measured ratio when available or the last confirmed ratio.
- With `focusLock`, continuous autofocus follows the region first, and `TextFocusCycle` decides when the camera's own locking scan runs, the same sweep and frame-proven lock the document camera uses through `FocusController`. Continuous autofocus reports focus it has not reached at close range, so its report is never trusted: on a steady hold, its settling starts a lock that confirms it, as its reporting no focus does, and so does a dark scene by the document camera's exposure × ISO signal with the torch off, since continuous autofocus is known to fail there; continuous autofocus that never settles locks after 0.6 s of a steady hold. The first settled lock permits reading, since unconfirmed continuous autofocus misreads at close range; after it, reading goes on while a lock is released, since the hand corrects the distance faster than a new scan, and pauses only during a lock's scan, which sweeps the lens. Under the lock, `TextMap` holds the text read around the aim: the region's centre is the selector and the region the tile each recognition reads. Every line a recognition finds is kept with its box, a line read again at the same place gains a read, and one the tile no longer finds goes; lines outside the tile keep theirs. Image flow on the nearest line carries the map between reads. During motion, loss of flow clears the map. The centre selects only within the bounds of a line with two agreeing reads out of its last four. On unagreed text or a gap, the reading clears. OCR runs when coverage changes or the aimed line still needs agreement. A tile that returned text is refreshed after 0.5 s, subject to the recognition rate. Lines expire after 1.5 s without a new OCR position or when they leave the aim's neighbourhood. OCR runs at most twice per second while moving and always respects `recognitionRate`. The lock's first reads run until the aimed line agrees or four reads have run. Main hands the analyzer a `ReadPermission`: the map's lock, which a released lock keeps, and an admission revision that changes with every change of permission, a scan that starts and fails back to the same lock included. Only frames exposed after a lock settles read under it, and a recognition or queued publication from another admission drops, so text read before a scan never shows or resolves `readText()` after it. `ManagedTextReading` owns the analyzer's side: the map, its sensor times, the lock's first-read verdict, the aimed line and its distance baseline, which is the mapped line itself, so moving onto the same text at another size measures afresh. The aim's selection is published once under each admission, empty ones included, so a publication main dropped across a change is repaired on the next frame. Paced and managed OCR each own their reading and tracking: main advances the analysis revision and clears the reading when `focusLock` starts or stops managed OCR, so a recognition in flight from the other mode and its queued highlights drop, and a quick toggle cannot keep the map; focus scans and releases within managed OCR keep it. A recognition republishes the aimed line with its renewed bounds, confidence and votes; between recognitions flow moves only the native highlight. A new lock clears the map. The reading decides only whether focus needs help, never where it is: text often reads while soft, so stopping at the first readable lens position pinned the lens at one distance. A lock under which 4 reads find no agreed text at the aim locks again. A hold gets 2 locks, so a view without text stops scanning. A turn of about 4 degrees, twice a steady hand's drift, 0.6 s of motion, longer than a tremor reads as motion, or the aimed line growing or shrinking by 15% since it was first measured under the lock, as moving nearer or farther does past the shallow depth of focus at reading distance, though a slow move straight back neither turns nor shakes the phone, releases the lock and starts the count over, as new text in the region does. The lock focuses and meters exposure at the region. It needs single-shot autofocus, realtime timestamps, a gyroscope and linear acceleration; otherwise, or when the motion sensors send nothing, continuous autofocus alone follows the region.
- A tap's scan holds the lens for 5 s, then continuous autofocus resumes and the focus cycle starts over. Zoom restarts the focus cycle, since it can switch lenses. Region changes release the lock and reset the cycle and the votes. Filter changes reset votes alone. `RecognitionPace` slows it to once a second after 4 recognitions in a row read the agreed text with the text still, and returns it to `recognitionRate` when a recognition disagrees, the tracked outline moves half its height or a tenth of its width, the phone turns, or a lock's scan ends. Votes also age out on each analyzed frame, not only on new recognitions, so a reading and its highlight end about 1.5 s after the last agreeing recognition even while recognitions are slow. The check runs on frames rather than a timer, so it can run late by however long no frame reaches the analyzer, as during a running recognition. Focus frames from a superseded cycle or region are dropped. [Host integration](../integration.md#text-reading) owns the public props and events.

## Samples

`SampleCollection` owns recording and `SampleFolders` owns persisted SAF access. Capture samples pin their admitted original and analysis provenance, saving the inference input and its raw prediction. User crop edits do not enter the prediction. The flat image/JSON format retains its legacy `quad` field for the [labeling tool](../tools.md).
