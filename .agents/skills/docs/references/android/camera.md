# Android camera

## CameraX and focus

- The camera view binds against the host Activity and suspends when inactive, detached or backgrounded. The permission owner works independently of views, refreshes on Activity resume and shares one foreground prompt. CameraX negotiates preview, analysis and still capture with a shared viewport.
- Admission reads one resolved prop snapshot. Hardware-dependent decisions use the bound configuration. Rebinding waits for the active capture. The view borrows its session.
- `FocusController` owns continuous, tap and capture focus. Focus targets the page's print spot: on the page's first steady, confirmed frame, analysis picks the third-by-third cell with the most luma gradient energy, leaving out the page's border and preferring the centre on near-ties, since a mostly blank page gives autofocus little to measure. The spot is kept in page coordinates, so the tracked outline carries it with the page; it is picked again for a page arriving anew, a restarted focus cycle, or a spot that left the frame. Before it is picked, the page centre stands in and no page lock starts; without a page, continuous AF targets the center. The focus box moves towards the page's centre, or shrinks, until it lies inside the outline, so the background behind a held page cannot take focus; a page too small or narrow for the smallest box gets none: no focus dot, continuous AF returns to the frame's centre rather than keep the page's last box, and its page lock meters at the centre. Luma for the spot and the sharpness score is BT.601 over the RGBA analysis frame's R, G and B, so coloured print and a tinted light score by brightness rather than one channel. The focus dot and sharpness square follow the spot every frame, but continuous AF is re-aimed only when it drifts a quarter of the box, because each region change can restart a search; a held lock is not moved. `Camera` turns `focusLock` on in document mode. With it, the scanner runs one focus cycle per steady hold, each step waiting for a steady hold: the capture's motion rule on optical flow, without its blur check, while a page is tracked or a lock holds, and otherwise the gyroscope and linear accelerometer both quiet for 300 ms, since a defocused, mostly blank frame's flow and blur readings track noise and would hold back the lock that makes it sharp; a gyroscope turn releases its lock and restarts the cycle. A confirmed page gets a page owner's lock at the focus box, held until the page is unseen for 1 s; the model's outline jitters and drops out on a still page, so it never ends a lock by itself. With no page found for 200 ms once the phone is steady, as when it is too blurred to detect, a recovery owner locks the frame's centre for 5 s, and its settling asks analysis for the next frame, since a search otherwise retries once a second while flow on a blurred frame reads as motion; a page found under it takes the lock over once its print spot is picked, so continuous AF never holds the lens between the two; if no page is found after it, or any lock fails, an auto torch lights for one more lock at the page or the centre, then the cycle gives up until the phone turns, the camera moves and holds steady again, or a page arrives anew. A scan's trigger the camera drops, seen as a single-shot frame reporting inactive after the trigger and before any scan or lock, is retried once for the scanner's own lock; the lock after lighting the torch waits for a frame the torch lit with exposure and white balance converged. While `focusLock` and live corners are on, the cycle owns every focus attempt for a press that requires focus (`ScannerPress`), within one 8 s budget from the press that runs on uptime, so it ends even if frames stop, and a cycle restarted by a new steady hold does not refill it. The shot starts no scan of its own: quality mode's per-photo scan and the stale-focus, dark and unseen-page scans apply only without the scanner's focus. The press resolves on a lock the cycle accepted (a page lock after its sharpness verdict, a centre lock only when no page is required, since with a page required it is progress toward the page, a tap's lock, or the final scan's), judged on a frame exposed after the lock, with at least 500 ms for that frame. A page found under the centre lock takes it over before the page's shot. After the final scan locks, the wait for fresh inference to confirm the page is bounded by the same budget. A press reads live corners as they are now, so a detector turned off mid-press leaves no page to wait for; output and processing keep the press's own settings. When the cycle gives up, or with 2 s of the budget left while it still waits, the press runs one final scan with the camera's single-shot autofocus at the page or the centre, unless a tap's lock or a shot holds the lens; a failed final scan or the spent budget fails the press with the usual document or focus timeout. Until then the page and focus waits hold past the shot's four-second focus deadline. The live setting decides, so turning `focusLock` or live corners off hands a waiting press to the regular checks on the current binding. A page lock's reported success is checked against the print: analysis scores the Laplacian variance of a square of luma at the focus aim, and `LockSharpnessJudge` keeps the page's best median of three counted frames (steady, exposure and white balance converged, autofocus not scanning). A lock whose next three frames score under 70% of that best missed, and counts as failed after one retry; with no best yet it is accepted, and a shot waits for the verdict. While any scan's successful lock holds, a frame reporting inactive still counts as focused. A tap or capture scan takes the lens meanwhile. With `focusLock`, a released lock leaves the lens where it stopped until the next lock: continuous AF on a mostly blank page moves it away from sharp, the next page is usually at about the same distance, and a new steady hold locks again. A camera started, as after a shot, opens with autofocus off at that camera's last successful lock distance, saved by camera id across launches, or 4 diopters before its first lock; lens units and holding distances differ between phones, cameras and people, so the distance is learned rather than fixed. A page lock then focuses itself with autofocus still off (`LensSearch`): with exposure locked so every position is scored alike and exposure still adjusting in a dim or flickering room cannot stall it, it averages two steady print scores at the lens's distance and 0.25 diopters either side, settling there unless a side scores over 4% higher, otherwise steps toward the sharper side until the score falls below 85% of the best or fails to lead twice, and settles at the leading position; the 4% margin keeps a flat score, as over blank paper, from wandering on noise. Meanwhile the focus dot shows scanning (yellow) and ripples at each lens step. Turning `focusLock` or live corners on or off sets or clears the held lens on the bound camera, ending any search; the change waits for a shot being taken to settle, and turning on waits for a tap's or capture's scan, so neither moves the lens under them. The auto torch is one lit state for a dark room and the cycle's torch step alike: the step sends a request a dark room already latched rather than give up with the torch off. Its command waits only while a shot is being taken, or for the whole pending capture with zero shutter lag, whose buffer refuses frames while white balance settles under new light; boost and the user's own torch mode still wait for the pending capture to settle. The trace records the torch request, CameraX's confirmation, and each frame's flash state. A position counts once a frame reports the lens there and still, scored from every analysed frame, including those where flow lost the page between inferences while it was seen within 1 s and both the gyroscope and the accelerometer covered the last 300 ms and report the hand quiet; its scores need the gyroscope and accelerometer quiet rather than flow steadiness, since each lens move changes the image's scale and blur, which flow reads as motion. The search skips the camera's own AF because the SM-G998U parks the lens at its near end on entering single-shot and contrast AF settles on false peaks over a mostly blank page. The first centre lock with a remembered distance keeps the lens there and settles at once. Tap and capture scans, the lit centre lock, a centre lock before any remembered distance, and a search that finds no settled score in 20 steady frames or no peak in 15 positions use the camera's single-shot AF instead: the app sets it as its own setting and sends the trigger once a frame reports single-shot, since the SM-G998U drops a trigger sent as the camera leaves another mode. Scanner mode thus runs no continuous AF, and the focus dot hides while the lens is held. `showFocusIndicator`, on by default, draws a small dot where focus is held, colored by its state. Continuous AF settles low-contrast pages short of sharp and drifts with the hand. The scanner claims tap ownership before requesting CameraX metering. A held tap lock counts even if the page arrived later. Other focus evidence is frame-scoped and must follow page arrival when a page is required.
- `quality` requests a shutter scan. Other modes can request one for stale focus or poor light. A failed capture scan gets at most one retry. A pending owned scan extends the wait; stale passive scanning frames expire. Motion checks begin once focus is accepted. On Camera2 REALTIME devices the gyro may supplement optical flow.

## Light and capture

- `CameraLightController` owns torch/boost requests and confirmations. It applies deferred settings after capture and ignores late completions after unbind. Exposure compensation also defers and uses the latest prop.
- Auto torch uses exposure × ISO and latches once lit. On supported cameras the boost setting is sent at bind and when changed. CameraX handles the interaction between torch and boost. For requested ZSL, a lit torch, sustained unsettled white balance or sustained darkness without boost can bind latency. So does `focusLock` with live corners, because its lens is held with autofocus off and CameraX buffers only frames reporting autofocus focused, so every gated ZSL shot would starve. CameraX buffers only frames with exposure and white balance converged; locked ones do not count. A gated ZSL shot rejected with only a buffered frame missing (the page steady when motion is checked, and a recent frame reporting focus, exposure and white balance the buffer refuses), as when flicker or moving shadows keep exposure adjusting, restarts the camera once in latency, which holds for the view's lifetime. The gate marks that rejection, and the restart waits for the capture to end, so a deactivation stops the camera instead. The reason is attached to the binding. For the torch, white-balance and darkness fallbacks, recovery returns to ZSL on a later bind; incomplete darkness/white-balance intervals do not count across camera stops.
- For gated ZSL, readiness includes a buffer-eligible frame even when focus is optional, with a fixed-focus exception. CameraX owns buffered frame selection. Skipping the gate skips the buffer check. See [Shared engine](../engine.md) for policy decisions.

## Text camera

`HybridTextCameraView` binds its own `LifecycleCameraController` with preview and RGBA analysis only. The preview fills the view, and the session config carries the preview's viewport explicitly, so the crop rect of each analysis frame is exactly what the view shows. `regionInBuffer` maps the region into that crop through the frame's rotation. The analysis thread copies just that crop, turns it upright, and runs ML Kit on it with no rotation, since ML Kit's own rotation shifts its boxes; then it votes. The outline restarts at the newest frame that read the agreed text. Between recognitions, the document guide's optical flow runs on every frame over the region plus a margin, and its transform moves the outline. Unknown motion leaves it where it is. Main publishes only results from the current binding and vote revision. Each binding observes its camera state: a critical error stops the camera and reports `camera/unavailable`. The camera closing, or a recoverable error while it opens, goes through the same transition as a stop: the reading clears, pending reads fail with `camera/unavailable` and queued frames stop counting. Frames are dropped until the camera is open again, and the first fresh frame restores `ready`. The filter and agreement rules in `TextLineReading.kt` have JVM tests that match iOS.

## Samples

`SampleCollection` owns recording and `SampleFolders` owns persisted SAF access. Capture samples pin their admitted original and analysis provenance, saving the inference input and its raw prediction. User crop edits do not enter the prediction. The flat image/JSON format retains its legacy `quad` field for the [labeling tool](../tools.md).
