# Android camera

## Document camera

- The view owns CameraX binding, capture admission and presentation. It borrows the host session. Camera use requires an active, attached foreground view. Preview, analysis and still capture share a viewport.
- Capture output and processing use the settings admitted at the press. Live focus and guide settings govern readiness. Rebinding waits for capture settlement.
- [Shared engine](../engine.md) owns guide acceptance and gate contracts. [Android validation](validation.md) owns hardware checks and trace workflows.

### Focus ownership

- `FocusController` owns lens commands and tap/capture ownership. The page print target is stored in page coordinates so flow can carry it. Metering fits inside the page. If a box cannot fit, focus uses the frame centre.
- With `focusLock` and live corners enabled, the scanner runs a recovery cycle per steady hold. A confirmed page can take the lens, and capture waits for the lock’s sharpness verdict. Without a page, a centre lock can sharpen the next inference. An eligible auto-torch step gives the cycle one more attempt before it gives up. A brief guide dropout does not release the page lock. Sustained loss or a phone turn of about 4 degrees, twice a steady hand's drift, can release it.
- The held lens retains its distance between locks. A camera starts at its remembered distance when available. Manual search compares print sharpness at locked exposure, and the camera's autofocus handles fallback. Both scanners' searches count a frame only when its capture request asked for the position being scored, since frames queued from the previous move can read within the arrival tolerance. A missing lens state proves no stop, so the reading must then repeat, and with no reading either, 3 frames under the move must pass. Searches end at a parabola's peak through the best position and its neighbours, within half a step, since the sharp zone is thinner than a step. Search can use a recent page's print samples during guide dropout when both motion sensors cover the window.
- The cycle owns focus for a press that requires it. `ScannerPress` provides one 8 s uptime budget, with one final autofocus attempt at give-up or near the deadline. A centre lock qualifies only when the press needs no page. Capture needs a fresh post-lock frame and, when required, fresh page evidence. Turning off managed focus hands a waiting press to the regular gate. Lens-setting changes defer during exposure.

### Light and ZSL

- `CameraLightController` owns torch and boost commands and confirmations. Auto torch latches for the run and shares that decision with focus recovery. With latency bound it may light while a press waits. With ZSL bound it waits for capture settlement. Manual torch, boost and exposure changes defer during a pending capture.
- CameraX owns ZSL frame selection. Gated ZSL checks buffer eligibility even with optional focus, subject to the fixed-focus exception. Held-lens focus, torch, sustained white-balance instability and darkness without boost can bind latency. A buffer-starved capture can trigger one latency fallback for the view lifetime. Other light fallbacks are reconsidered on later bindings.

## Text camera

- `HybridTextCameraView` owns a separate preview and RGBA analysis binding. The shared viewport maps the visible OCR region into the analysis crop. ML Kit receives upright pixels. Optical flow carries the newest agreed outline between recognitions.
- Main publishes a per-frame snapshot of region, filter, vote revision, zoom admission and focus session. The analyzer owns voting. Main accepts only current binding and revision results. Closing, interruption or failure clears readings and fails pending reads with `camera/unavailable`. A fresh frame restores readiness on reopen.
- `TextZoom` separates the submitted ratio from camera evidence. Each request clears the reading and pauses recognition and focus scoring. The current request's completion and matching capture metadata establish the frame boundary. When matching metadata is unavailable or does not arrive within the grace period, admission uses the next frame after the latest observed result. Refusal uses the measured ratio when available or the last confirmed ratio.
- `TextLensFocus` owns the lens search and held sharpness evidence. It requires manual lens control, realtime timestamps, gyro and linear acceleration. Unsupported configurations and missing sensor evidence fall back to camera autofocus. Search locks exposure and compares region sharpness. Only a positive peak is saved, keyed by camera and settled zoom. A search compares the region's fine detail, the Laplacian variance, across lens positions. A hold judges focus by the ratio of fine detail to coarse detail, the same variance over 2 by 2 blocks, because other text sliding into the region changes both while defocus takes fine detail first. Its focus staying under 70% of the held level for 8 steady frames searches again. Frames with under 5% of the held coarse detail are not judged, since a bare region scores sensor noise. Coarse detail rising past 2.5 times the level searches a hold without a lock, as after blank paper, and takes a new level for a lock. A turn of about 4 degrees, twice a steady hand's drift, only checks a lock: close-up text after a turn is usually at the same distance, so 3 steady frames at the held distance keep it unless their focus is under 70% of the earlier level.
- A tap temporarily takes lens ownership. Zoom replaces the focus session. Region changes reset search evidence and votes at the current lens distance. Filter changes reset votes alone. `RecognitionPace` slows it to once a second after 4 recognitions in a row read the agreed text with the text still, and returns it to `recognitionRate` when a recognition disagrees, the tracked outline moves half its height or a tenth of its width, the phone turns, or a search ends. Votes also age out on each analyzed frame, not only on new recognitions, so a reading and its highlight end about 1.5 s after the last agreeing recognition even while recognitions are slow. The check runs on frames rather than a timer, so it can run late by however long no frame reaches the analyzer, as during a running recognition. Focus samples from superseded holds or regions are dropped. [Host integration](../integration.md#text-reading) owns the public props and events.

## Samples

`SampleCollection` owns recording and `SampleFolders` owns persisted SAF access. Capture samples pin their admitted original and analysis provenance, saving the inference input and its raw prediction. User crop edits do not enter the prediction. The flat image/JSON format retains its legacy `quad` field for the [labeling tool](../tools.md).
